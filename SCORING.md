# How WoWBest scores gear

WoWBest selects the character’s class, talent profile and level before scoring. The planner and item tooltips use the same weights. Damage profiles favor their normal attacks and spells. Healing profiles favor group healing and mana sustain. Protection and Bear balance survival with threat. The weights are expressed in profile points. An upgrade percentage measures the change in the score of the equipped set. Actual DPS, healing and time to kill depend on the encounter and the player.

## A level-19 Warrior

Strength grants 2 attack power per point. A level-19 Warrior gains approximately one percentage point of physical critical strike chance from 7.4 Agility, compared with 20 Agility at level 60. Agility also grants 2 armor per point. For Arms, +5 Strength and +10 attack power therefore score equally. Protection gives Strength a little extra credit for shield block value. Arms and Fury also value Spirit during leveling for health regeneration between pulls. [Forever client conversions by level](https://foreverdb.net/stats/warrior) · [Warrior leveling stats](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/profile/ahlaundoh-flnv45/classes/warrior-leveling-guide)

| Input at level 19 | Arms points | Fury points | Protection points |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| +1 Strength | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.51 |
| +1 Agility | 0.48 | 0.44 | 0.60 |
| +1 Stamina | 0.36 | 0.36 | 1.40 |
| +1 Spirit | 0.27 | 0.27 | 0.08 |
| +1 main-hand weapon DPS | 6.20 | 5.70 | 3.80 |
| +1 average main-hand hit damage, at equal DPS | 0.62 | 0.14 | 0.00 |

For example, **Smite’s Mighty Hammer** has 19.71 DPS, 55–83 damage, 11 Strength and 4 Agility. Its base Arms item score is about 177.91 points at level 19. **Cruel Barb**, with 15.54 DPS, 30–57 damage and 12 attack power, scores about 129.32 in the main hand. A shield or another legal off-hand changes the total. The hammer uses both hands. Protection evaluates one-handed setups with a shield. Fury can use a two-hander while leveling, and the client must say the character can dual wield before a second weapon can enter the set. ## What changes with the character

- **Defense on tanks.** The Forever client conversion table gives one Defense skill per point of Defense rating throughout levels 1–60. One extra skill above the character’s normal maximum grants 0.04 percentage points each of miss, dodge, parry and block where applicable, and reduces incoming critical strikes. WoWBest applies the same weight to the rating or skill form of an item stat, counts miss like dodge, and assigns Bear zero credit for parry or block. The score omits a separate value for critical-strike suppression because the enemy and the tank’s distance from a critical-immunity threshold can vary. [WoWSims client-derived conversion](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/1b149a63f75ef150a4f657392279c81810dbf555/sim/core/base_stats_auto_gen.go#L13) · [Build 70790 rating table](https://wowforever.kickegg.net/articles/forever-combat-ratings-conversion?lang=en) · [Defense effects in the Forever client](https://foreverdb.net/stats/warrior)
- **Level and attributes.** The profile uses the Forever client’s rounded Agility-to-crit and Intellect-to-spell-crit values at the character’s exact level. Hit, crit, haste and expertise rating keep their game units. For example, 14 crit rating equals one percentage point. The *score* of a percentage grows with the modeled value of the attacks or heals it affects. WoWBest multiplies endgame throughput-rating weights by `0.12 + 0.88 × (level / 60)²`. This interpolation, the flat point weights and the relative importance of sustain are WoWBest tuning choices. The gear score applies linear hit and expertise values, with no assumptions about a particular enemy’s level or a character’s remaining hit to cap. [Class and level tables](https://foreverdb.net/stats) · [Forever combat ratings](https://wowforever.kickegg.net/articles/forever-combat-ratings-conversion?lang=en)
- **Physical damage.** Main-hand DPS matters for weapon users. Arms, most Rogues and Retribution also get credit for average hit damage on their weapon-based attacks. Fury gives more weight to the off hand and critical strikes as its later abilities become available. Beast Mastery and Marksmanship emphasize the ranged weapon. Survival emphasizes melee weapon damage and retains a smaller contribution for shots. [Arms](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/arms-warrior-guide) · [Fury pre-raid benchmark and assumptions](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/fury-warrior-guide) · [Retribution](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/retribution/stat-priority-pve-dps) · [Survival](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/survival-hunter-melee-dps-pve-stat-priority)
- **Feral forms.** In Forever the base DPS of Cat and Bear autoattacks uses the equipped weapon’s DPS. Form swing times are normalized to 1.0 seconds for Cat and 2.5 for Bear. Two weapons with equal DPS and different swing times receive equal weapon-damage points in these profiles. Cat gains attack power from Agility once Cat Form becomes available at level 20. Bear favors armor, health and dodge, and receives zero block or parry points. [Blizzard’s Hunter and Druid deep dive](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-%E2%80%94-hunter-and-druid/2367361) · [Feral DPS stats](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/feral-dps-druid/bis-gear-and-stats) · [Cat Form while leveling](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/feral-cat-druid-leveling-guide-for-wow-forever-35077)
- **Casters and healers.** Early Shadow Priest profiles value wand DPS, Spirit and mana between pulls. The wand weight falls as the class gains its spell toolkit. Frost and Fire gear receive different scores by spec. Destruction favors Fire damage, with a smaller Shadow contribution. Generic spell power works across schools. Healing roles value healing power, Intellect and mana regeneration, with separate weights for Spirit and spell crit. Bonus spell damage alone does not increase the healing profile’s score. [Shadow leveling](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/priest/shadow/dps-leveling-tips) · [Frost Mage](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/frost/stat-priority-pve-dps) · [Destruction](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/destruction/stat-priority-pve-dps) · [Holy Priest](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/holy-priest/bis-gear-and-stats) · [Holy Paladin leveling](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/holy-paladin-leveling)
- **Hybrid effects.** Shaman Enhancement and Paladin Retribution and Protection can benefit from both physical and spell effects. When an effect explicitly affects only one type of attack, WoWBest uses the corresponding share of the profile’s unified crit value. A group-healing profile can benefit from spell crit while gaining zero from offensive spell hit. School contributions and hybrid shares are assumptions for the named playstyle. Pet choice, the character’s actual spell mix and individual talent ranks can change their relative value. [Enhancement](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/shaman/enhancement/overview-pve-dps) · [Demonology’s alternate fire and shadow builds](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/demonology/stat-priority-pve-dps)
